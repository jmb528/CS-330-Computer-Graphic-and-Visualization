# CS-330 Computer Graphics and Visualization

## How do I approach designing software?

Throughout this course, I learned that designing software requires planning, patience, and being willing to make adjustments when something doesn't work as expected. Working on my 3D scene helped me better understand how different components, such as textures, lighting, objects, and camera controls, work together. My approach was to break the project into smaller parts instead of trying to complete everything at once. Going forward, I can apply this same approach to other projects by focusing on organization, testing, and making improvements throughout development.

## How do I approach developing programs?

When developing programs, I prefer working step by step and testing my code as I go. Throughout this project, I used C++ and OpenGL to create and improve my 3D scene. Each milestone introduced something new, whether it was building objects, applying textures, adjusting lighting, or implementing camera movement. There were times when something didn't work the way I expected, so I had to troubleshoot and make changes until I got the results I wanted. This experience helped me become more comfortable with debugging and showed me how important it is to build on what I've already completed.

## How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?

Computer science continues to help me develop skills that I can use throughout my education and future career. Before taking this course, I didn't have much experience working with computational graphics, but now I have a better understanding of how 3D environments are created and rendered. Although my main career interests are in cybersecurity and software development, the problem-solving, programming, and debugging skills I practiced in this course are useful in both fields. This project also reminded me that learning something unfamiliar can be challenging, but working through those challenges helps me become a better developer.
